Practice the operational lifecycle rather than only model architecture:

1. [Feature pipelines and parity](../../docs/04-ai-ml-llms/20-feature-pipelines-and-parity.md)

For each lab, state what is measured, what can leak or fail, and which
production controls are missing. Keep retrieval evidence separate from claims
of factual correctness, and keep offline quality separate from online safety
and latency.
